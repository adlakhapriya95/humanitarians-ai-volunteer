- **Pipeline coding & agnostic architecture (6.0 hrs).**
  Refactored pipeline components in `rlcd/` to make the system fully data- and
  model-agnostic:
  1. *Generic Data Adapter (`processing.py`):* Built `auto_prepare_dataset_for_laya()`
     to dynamically detect and adapt incoming schemas across function-calling
     (xLAM), clinical emergency triage, native Laya formats, and generic NLP
     classification (auto-mapping prompt/text and target/label columns) with
     custom `column_map` support.
  2. *3-Way Dataset Split (`main.py`):* Replaced all-train ingestion with
     reproducible 3-way partitioning (`split_dataset_items`): Train (70%),
     Calibration holdout (10% for Laya temperature scaling), and Test (20%
     held-out for unbiased metric benchmarking).
  3. *Active Hyperparameters & Tuning Sweep (`main.py`):* Exposed all training
     parameters (`micro_batch_size`, `grad_accum_steps`, `group_size`, `lr_head`,
     `lr_encoder`, `sigma_start`, `sigma_end`, `ce_weight`, `reward_strategy`).
     Built `hyperparameter_sweep()` utility to test candidate parameter grids and
     rank configurations by a composite calibration score ($\text{Brier} - 0.5 \times \text{ECE}$).
