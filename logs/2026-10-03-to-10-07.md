- **Pipeline coding & agnostic architecture (6.0 hrs).**
  Refactored pipeline components in `rlcd/` to make the system fully data- and
  model-agnostic:
  1. *Generic Data Adapter (`processing.py`):* Built `auto_prepare_dataset_for_laya()`

     reproducible 3-way partitioning (`split_dataset_items`): Train (70%),

     Built `hyperparameter_sweep()` utility to test candidate parameter grids and
     rank configurations by a composite calibration score ($\text{Brier} - 0.5 \times \text{ECE}$).
