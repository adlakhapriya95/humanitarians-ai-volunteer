  1. *Mathematical Root Cause Analysis & Empirical Discovery:*

     - Diagnosed why Expected Calibration Error (ECE) widened on subjective tasks (Tweet Sentiment and Anthropic RLHF) despite massive accuracy surges (+32% to +35%): standard Cross-Entropy loss ($-\log p$) drives output logits toward $+\infty$ on hard one-hot targets (`[1.0, 0.0, 0.0]`), conflicting with inherent human annotator variance and causing models to become overconfident bluffers.
  2. *Post-Hoc Calibration Engine & Empirical Bake-Off:*
     - Developed a standalone, reproducible post-hoc calibration engine (`rlcd/run_post_hoc_calibration.py`) supporting Temperature Scaling, Platt/Vector Scaling, and Matrix Scaling with L-BFGS optimization.
     - Executed a head-to-head empirical bake-off on held-out calibration splits:
       - *Platt / Vector Scaling ($A \cdot z + b$):* Won on multi-class choice tasks, cutting ECE from $9.49\% \to 5.29\%$ (44% reduction) and improving Brier score from $-0.6059 \to -0.5189$.
       - *Temperature Scaling ($T^*$):* Proved mathematically optimal for pairwise preference tasks (Anthropic RLHF), preserving logit antisymmetry without introducing positional bias.
  3. *Rescuing the Three Subjective Failure Rows:*
