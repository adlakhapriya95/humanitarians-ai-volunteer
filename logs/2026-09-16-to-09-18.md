- **Project decision and Gemma study (4 hrs).**
  Chose the Gemma track and am building around [FunctionGemma 270M](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/functiongemma/model_card).
  The project is *"Fine-tune FunctionGemma 270M for tool selection."* The research
  question is whether a model this small can choose tools reliably enough to take
  tool routing away from the main agent. Chose it for three reasons:
  1. It is a 270M model meant to be fine-tuned for one narrow task, allowing training
     and testing on modest hardware without a large GPU setup.
  2. Google publishes a dataset and fine-tuning recipe giving a working baseline
     (model card reports 58% before fine-tuning and 85% after on Mobile Actions).
  3. Success can be checked automatically by running the selected tool in a sandbox,
     with an explicit no-tool option fitting the Optimus evaluation loop.
  
  Read the [FunctionGemma formatting guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/functiongemma/formatting-and-best-practices)
  and [fine-tuning guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/functiongemma/finetuning-with-functiongemma).
  Also read the [Gemma 4 overview](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/core),
  [function calling page](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/capabilities/text/function-calling-gemma4),
  and [Unsloth training guide](https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/gemma-4/train) to see
